1. What breaks today
1Guest entry at the front desk is typed by hand: 3 to 4 minutes per guest.
2Bookings arrive on WhatsApp, phone and OTA with no sync between them.
3Double bookings happen because nothing stops the second one.
4Tasks between desks are coordinated verbally. Nothing is written down or timed.
5VIP bookings bypass the system and live in someone's phone.
2. How we will know it worked
Check-in takes under 45 seconds
Zero double bookings in the first 90 days
80% or more of arrival-to-checkout tasks are created automatically
3. In scope
Guests, bookings, rooms, bills and tasks across all departments
AI draft from WhatsApp messages, reviewed by the front desk
Staff phone for housekeeping, bell desk and security
Tax bills with VAT 13%, credit notes, reprints
