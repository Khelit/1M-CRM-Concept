Booking rules

Overbooking
Not allowed (enforced by the database)
No setting turns this off. Two bookings can never hold the same room on the same night.
Hold expiry
24 h
A hold with no deposit is released after this. See the hold-expiry rule in Handoff rules.
Room assigned
At check-in for pooled types
Named type (Courtyard Suite 503, 504) is assigned when booked.
Pooled room types. A room in a pooled type is normally assigned at check-in. The front desk may assign it earlier, inside the Confirm details task, so Prepare room has a room number 24 h before arrival.
